6Quick answers

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

It sounds natural, and we're upfront about it. If a caller asks for a person, or the question is outside what it knows, it transfers the call to you or takes a message and texts it to you right away.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. We forward calls to the AI only when you want, for example after hours, when you don't pick up, or all the time.

Who owns my website and ad accounts?

You do. Ads run on your own Google and Meta accounts with your card, and your domain stays in your name.

Is there a contract?

Plans start with 6 months so the work has time to show results. After that you can stay month to month or cancel any time.

Can you work in Spanish?

Yes. The phone agent, website and ads can all run in English and Spanish.
